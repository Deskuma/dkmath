## Part B — unique prime above seven in the real cubic ring of integers

Let

    thetaI :=
      SevenRealCubic.modelEquivRingOfIntegers
        SevenRealCubicInt.eisensteinAxis

and

    P7 := Ideal.span {thetaI}.

Transport:

    SevenRealCubicInt.eisensteinAxis_prime

and

    7 = eisensteinAxis^3 * thetaSevenUnit

to the actual ring of integers.

Prove:

    P7.IsPrime
    P7.IsMaximal

and the ideal identity

    Ideal.span {(7 : O Field)} = P7^3.

The unit factor must disappear only through the checked fact that the
principal ideal of a unit is top.

Then prove uniqueness:

For every prime/maximal ideal P lying over (7),

    P = P7.

Preferred proof:
- P lies over (7), hence 7 belongs to P;
- equivalently P divides the principal ideal (7);
- use (7)=P7^3 and P prime to show P divides P7;
- maximality/properness gives equality.

Expose:

    (Ideal.primesOver (Ideal.span {(7 : Z)}) (O Field)).ncard = 1.

## Part C — seven is absent from each square-root norm

## Part F — the defining cubic splits in Field

Let:

    theta0 := SevenRealCubic.powerBasis.gen
    theta1 := fieldRotateEquiv theta0
    theta2 := fieldRotateEquiv theta1.

Prove:
- polynomialQ.eval₂ ... thetai = 0 for i=0,1,2;
- theta0, theta1, theta2 are pairwise distinct.

The root statements should follow because fieldRotateEquiv is a Q-algebra
automorphism and theta0 is a root of its minpoly.

Since:

    polynomialQ.Monic
    polynomialQ.natDegree = 3,

prove that polynomialQ splits over Field.

Any clean Mathlib route is acceptable:
- explicit factorization into the three linear factors;
- root-cardinality plus monicity/natDegree;
- a splitting-field constructor.

Do not use numerical approximations.

## Part H — complete splitting of every common norm prime

Let q be prime and assume q divides both square-root norms.

R29 gives:

    2 <= ncard(primesOver (q) (O Field)).

Under IsGalois Q Field use the standard Galois decomposition formula:

    ncard(primesOver (q))
      * ramificationIdxIn
      * inertiaDegIn
      =
    Nat.card Gal(Field/Q).

Use:

    IsGalois.card_aut_eq_finrank
    SevenRealCubic.finrank_eq_three

to identify the right side with 3.

All three factors are positive. Therefore:

    ncard(primesOver (q)) = 3
    ramificationIdxIn = 1
    inertiaDegIn = 1.

Do not assume unramifiedness first; it follows here from the product and
ncard >= 2.

Package the main theorem:

    common_norm_prime_splits_completely

with exact names convenient for downstream use.
