## Reuse decisions

- The exact gcd proof cancels 2 using Odd.coprime_two_left and Nat.Coprime.gcd_mul_left_cancel. It does not add a valuation proof.
- centeredOddGapProduct is an abbrev of Nat.doubleFactorial (2n-1). The existing odd product starts at 3 and uses a shifted length, so a shell-indexed thin bridge is needed; it retains the empty product convention.
- Wallis central and mirror half-products are rational ratios with oddLeftQ and oddRightQ. They are not duplicated or imported into Legendre. Mathlib factorial splitting gives the neutral Nat bridge directly.
- Existing finitePrimeBasisProduct and primeScalesUpTo express the odd radical without division by 2 or a competing primorial type.
- Nat.factorization_gcd and factorization_prod_apply give exact multiplicities for nonzero factors. factorization_def converts prime coordinates to padicValNat without a heavy analytic import.
- padicValNat.mul and padicValNat.pow in PadicVal/Basic were audited, but factorization is the smaller dependency route. Factorial valuation summation is available but a new factorial theory is unnecessary.
- Existing shifted homogeneous cyclotomic evaluator and primeOrder are reused. Phi_4 has index 4 and polynomial degree 2; the fixed shape follows from cyclotomic_prime_pow_eq_geom_sum. No cyclotomic field extension is added.
- Full-cover packet assumptions remain outside the unconditional norm/gap arithmetic; old-support disjointness retains the fresh-prime alternative.
