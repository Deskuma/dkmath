## Next-frontier source audit

GnomonPascalCell_mul_factorial is the exact shell product identity.
WallisCellGrowth.pascalCellGrowthQ_eq_cast_choose is an exact general-cell
receiver; its certified Wallis lower bounds primarily address central cells.
Far-edge gnomon cells require their full offset/prefix factors, so a central
estimate alone is not a shell psi lower bound. Nat.pow_le_choose gives a general
multiplicative lower bound, but it does not remove lower-divisor weights.
ArithmeticFunction.vonMangoldt_sum identifies log(q) with the sum of Lambda(d)
over divisors d of q. Summing this over shell labels is the smallest exact bridge.
Chebyshev.psi_ge and theta_ge are global lower bounds; subtracting a global psi
upper bound at the lower endpoint is valid but gives no positive short-shell
estimate with the audited constants. Bertrand central-binomial factorization
bounds control a doubling interval and do not transfer directly to square shells.

Proposed 028 identity: log(cell)+log((2*n)!) = shellPsiMass + C(n), where C(n)
is the finite sum over 1<=d<=n^2 of (top/d-n^2/d)*Lambda(d), using natural floor
division and real-cast multiplicities. Divisors above n^2 occur once in this
shell and constitute the shell mass; lower divisors must stay explicit.
This identity and any useful C upper bound are not implemented in 027.
The relation C-log((2*n)!)=oldCarryBudget-higherCorrection follows formally
from the two exact decompositions if the proposed identity is proved; it shows
why renaming this term alone cannot supply the missing lower-mass provider.
