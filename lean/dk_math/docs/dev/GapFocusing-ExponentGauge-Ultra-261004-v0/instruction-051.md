  P D q = 448*s^49,
  P D q = 7*q^6+35*D^2*q^4+21*D^4*q^2+D^6,
  D=7^27*r^49,  M=r*s,

with divisor support, coprimality, seven-unit data, parity, and

The critical remaining issue is not another modular/size filter. It is
whether the actual FLT7 source supplies a new natural additive chart,
and thus makes the exact centered equation mandatory, or supplies a
direct contradiction independently of reconstruction.

Treat this as a bounded source-to-target audit and stopping checkpoint.

## Primary audit: is there a source-derived center?

Trace all source equations that constrain the actual inner root and
the selected cyclotomic residual generator.

For each plausible source-defined choice of a factor allocation r
and centered coordinate q, check explicitly:

1. Is r a positive divisor of the actual M, with s=M/r and coprimality?
2. Is q a natural center of the correct parity relative to D?
3. Which canonical branch (q>D or q<D) is indicated by a proved
   source equation, and are the corresponding positive primitive
   natural endpoints constructible?
4. Most importantly, what proves the **exact** identity
       P D q = 448*s^49 ?
5. Does that identity come from the original source packet itself,
   rather than from assuming a Fermat chart, centered candidate,
   reconstruction obligation, or FLT7 conclusion?

The fifth point is mandatory. A norm identity, ideal equality,
seventh-power equality up to a root of unity, or 7-adic valuation
equality does not automatically give the additive identity.

If a thin genuine source-to-centered map exists, formalize it by
reusing the existing reverse reconstruction theorem and expose the
resulting genuine away counterexample and depth drop.

Do not introduce a parallel chart packet or merely define a new
structure containing the missing identity as a field.

## Alternative: a source-derived arithmetic exclusion

If direct construction fails, inspect whether the existing source
equations place r,s in the 050 no-image family or yield an independent
source-derived adjacent-polynomial bracket.

Possible substantive targets:

- prove a source-indexed selected allocation has s=t^6 and t>=9*r^2;
- prove a source-indexed arithmetic contradiction using the actual
  norm, prime-ownership, or coprime routing data;
- derive a new exact source-only constraint that demonstrably
  excludes a previously admissible factor allocation.

Do not infer an integer sixth power from a sixth-power **residue**,
nor from an algebraic normalizedAxis^6 factor in a ring identity.
Do not assume all r divisors satisfy a favorable premise.

Before claiming terminal exclusion, check the logic: proving the
reconstruction receiver empty without a proved implication forcing
reconstruction from the original source obstructs the proposed
descent method, not the existence of the original FLT7 source.
