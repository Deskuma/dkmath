## 1. What is already exact

For fixed `epsilon > 0`, the centered box multiplier is the logarithmic average

$$
H_\varepsilon(z)=\frac{1}{2\varepsilon}\int_{-\varepsilon}^{\varepsilon}e^{tz}\,dt.
$$

The current one-variable explicit-formula weight is retained separately as

$$
q_\varepsilon(z)=z^2H_\varepsilon(z).
$$

The new two-variable kernel candidate is

$$
K_\varepsilon(z,w)=z\overline{w}\,H_\varepsilon(z+\overline{w}).
$$

Its diagonal is therefore

$$
K_\varepsilon(z,z)=|z|^2H_\varepsilon(z+\overline z).
$$

This is intentionally not identified with `q_epsilon(z)`.

That separation is correct and should remain explicit.

---

## 2. The feature-map positivity is genuine

The module defines the finite-family real energy

$$
\mathcal G_\varepsilon(z,c)=\frac{1}{2\varepsilon}\int_{-\varepsilon}^{\varepsilon}\left|\sum_j c_j z_j e^{tz_j}\right|^2dt.
$$

and proves

$$
0\le\mathcal G_\varepsilon(z,c).
$$

This nonnegativity is independent of RH, zero-side defect positivity, finite prime-cutoff sign assumptions, and limit exchange.

So a genuine positive structure has been found.

The remaining issue is not positivity of the feature-map energy.  The remaining issue is exact identification of this energy with the quadratic form of the named kernel.

## 5. The key mismatch remains real

Even after PSD certification, the current one-variable explicit-formula weight and the Gram diagonal remain different theorem surfaces.

The current weight evaluates the multiplier at `z`:

$$
q_\varepsilon(z)=z^2H_\varepsilon(z).
$$

The Gram diagonal evaluates it at `z + conj z`:

$$
K_\varepsilon(z,z)=|z|^2H_\varepsilon(z+\overline z).
$$

Thus positivity of the Gram kernel does not imply positivity of the current linear explicit-formula surface by rewriting alone.

This is the central Gate 4B.3 gap.

## 8. Integration-by-parts caution
