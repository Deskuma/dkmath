## Coprimality argument

For `IsCoprime a b` and `7 | b`, suppose a prime `q` divides both `x` and
`sigma(x)`. It divides their difference `theta*alpha*b`.

- If `q | alpha`, then `q` divides a unit, contradicting primality.
- If `q | theta`, then the identity
  `theta^3 = -7*(theta+1)^2` and the unit property of `theta+1` imply
  `q | 7`; hence `q | b`.
- The remaining product branch already gives `q | b`.

Since `q | x = a+b*alpha`, it follows that `q | a`. Mapping the integer
Bezout certificate for `a,b` into the cubic ring makes `q` a unit, again a
contradiction.

Applying the order-three automorphism to this result proves all three pairs
among `x`, `sigma(x)`, and `sigma^2(x)` are coprime.

## Power extraction

The conjugate product is regrouped as:

```text
x * (sigma(x)*sigma^2(x)) = z^7.
```

The first factor is coprime to the second. Mathlib's
`exists_associated_pow_of_mul_eq_pow'` then gives:

```text
Norm(x) = z^7
  -> exists unit root, x = unit*root^7.
```

For the RAMIFIED packet:

```text
left coordinates  = (a,-n)
right coordinates = (a+n,n)
n = 7^4*m^7.
```

The existing primitive quadratic-root theorem supplies both integer Bezout
certificates. Therefore:

```text
etaL = uL*xiL^7
etaR = uR*xiR^7.
```

The new `RamifiedRealCubicUpToUnitPacket` stores these witnesses together
with:

```text
uR*xiR^7 - uL*xiL^7 =
  normalizedAxis^6*normalizedWitness^7.
```

## Inference and next prediction

The class-group and source-coprimality problems are closed. The exact
remaining obstruction is the pair of units.

Reduction modulo `7` is expected to expose

```text
O_K/(7) = F_7[tau]/(tau^3),
tau = theta mod 7.
```

The immediately testable first layer is:

```text
tau^3 = 0
every seventh power reduces to a scalar
alpha reduces to 3+tau
1+alpha reduces to 4+tau.
```

Those are necessary reduction facts. They are not yet sufficient to conclude
a global unit is a seventh power. Sufficiency additionally requires a formal
global unit quotient statement: the proposed `49` classes generated by
`alpha` and `1+alpha` must be exhaustive, or an equivalent unit-rank and
index theorem must be proved.

Once RAMIFIED-011U closes that exact bridge, each displayed unit can be
absorbed into its root, producing exact source seventh powers for
RAMIFIED-012. Only after that may the depth `13 = 10 + 3` calculation and
axis exponent drop be started in RAMIFIED-013.
