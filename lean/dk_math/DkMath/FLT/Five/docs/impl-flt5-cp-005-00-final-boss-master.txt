======================================================================

This is intentionally harder than a routine implementation task.

Do not wait for a human to supply every next lemma.  After each proved layer:

1. inspect the new exact equations;
2. derive candidate invariants, parity restrictions, valuation restrictions, and
   coprime factorizations;
3. test candidate statements on finite data only to reject false claims quickly;

5. prove it;
6. use it to generate the next packet or contradiction;
7. continue until Stop A or an exact Stop B.

The objective is not to reproduce a prewritten historical proof line by line.  The
objective is to let the compiled DkMath structure reveal the shortest non-circular
closure available in the current library.
