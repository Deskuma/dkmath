5. cp-004j-b — Prove the golden unit classes modulo fifth powers
======================================================================

Target the existing contract directly:

```lean
GoldenUnitClassesModFifth
```

That is, prove for every golden unit `epsilon`:

```text
epsilon = phi^i * delta^5
```

for some `i : Fin 5` and `delta : GoldenInt`.

The sign requires no extra sector because `-1 = (-1)^5`.

Investigate and attempt the following routes in order. Select the shortest route that is
fully kernel-certified.

Route A — elementary unit-coordinate descent
---------------------------------------------

For `x = (a,b)`, multiplication by `phi` and `phi^-1 = phi - 1` acts explicitly on
coordinates:

```text
(a,b) * phi      = (b, a+b)
(a,b) * phi^-1   = (b-a, a)
```

A unit satisfies:

```text
a^2 + a*b - b^2 = ±1
```

Develop a well-founded descent on a natural coordinate measure such as
`natAbs a + natAbs b`, proving that every non-base unit can be multiplied by one of
`phi`, `phi^-1`, or `-1` to reach a strictly smaller unit. Classify the finite base cases.

It is acceptable to first prove all units are signed integral powers of `phi`, then reduce
the exponent modulo five. It is also acceptable to make the descent accumulate the
exponent modulo five directly, avoiding a separate integer-exponent representation.

Route B — Pell/Fibonacci classification
---------------------------------------

Use the exact identity:

```text
(2*a+b)^2 - 5*b^2 = 4 * goldenNorm(a,b)
```

A unit therefore gives the generalized Pell equations with right side `±4`. Search
Mathlib and DkMath for Pell, Fibonacci, Lucas, metallic-ratio, and silver/golden unit
classification lemmas. If the missing bridge is small, prove it and recover signed powers
of `phi`.

Route C — a packet-sufficient substitute
----------------------------------------

If a global classification is disproportionately expensive, prove a theorem strictly
weaker than `GoldenUnitClassesModFifth` but sufficient to transform every packet witness

```text
beta = epsilon * gamma^5
```

into one of the five explicit representatives. The resulting theorem must produce an
actual equality in `GoldenInt`, not merely a residue statement.

Do not stop after finding a promising route. Implement it. If all three routes expose the
same precise missing proposition, isolate that complete proposition and add receivers,
but continue with any sector arithmetic that does not depend on it.



7. cp-004j-d — Zero-sector arithmetic kernel
======================================================================

Assume a packet `p`, `gamma = (r,s)`, and

```text
p.beta = gamma^5.
```

Use names that do not collide with the packet's existing `a` and `b` fields.

Derive the exact equations:

```text
N(gamma) = ±b
5 * s * H(r,s) = -5^7 * a^10
s * H(r,s) = -5^6 * a^10
```

where

```text
H(r,s) = r^4 + 2*r^3*s + 4*r^2*s^2 + 3*r*s^3 + s^4.
```

Prove the congruence:

```text
H(r,s) ≡ N(gamma)^2 (mod 5).
```

Since `5` does not divide `N(gamma)`, conclude:

```text
not (5 divides H(r,s)).
```

Next prove that the two coordinates of `gamma` are primitive. A suggested prime-divisor
argument is:

1. a common prime divisor of `r` and `s` divides `N(gamma)`, hence divides `b`;
2. its fifth power divides both coordinates of `gamma^5`, hence divides
   `beta.snd = -5^7*a^10`;
3. `Coprime a b` excludes every prime shared with `a`;
4. `not (5 divides b)` excludes the exceptional prime five.

Package the result in a form useful for the quartic factor, for example:

```text
Coprime (natAbs r) (natAbs s)
```

or equivalent integer gcd facts.

Then prove:

```text
gcd(natAbs s, natAbs H(r,s)) = 1
```

because `H(r,s) ≡ r^4 (mod s)` and the coordinates are primitive.

Transport the signed equation to a natural-number product equation, preserving all sign
information separately:

```text
natAbs s * natAbs H(r,s) = 5^6 * a^10.
```

Use coprimality and `5 ∤ H` to extract the strongest exact power split available. The
expected shape is:

```text
natAbs s = 5^6 * c^10
natAbs H(r,s) = d^10
```

with the remaining factors of `a` distributed compatibly between `c` and `d`.

Do not assert this shape without proving the required coprimality, prime-exponent, and
sign lemmas. Reuse existing natural fifth/tenth-power splitting APIs where possible.

Publish the useful intermediate facts. They are part of the proof, not documentation.

Suggested module:

```text
DkMath/FLT/Five/SignedGoldenZeroSector.lean
```

13. Mathematical compass
======================================================================

The intended compression is:

```text
beta = epsilon * gamma^5
  -> unit modulo fifth powers
  -> five explicit sectors
  -> sectors 1..4 force 5 | N(gamma)
  -> contradiction with N(gamma) = ±b and 5 ∤ b
  -> sector 0: beta = gamma^5
  -> s * H(r,s) = -5^6*a^10
  -> primitive coordinates and coprime factors
  -> exact tenth-power split
  -> finite obstruction or strict descent
  -> SignedGoldenUnitFifthPowerExclusion
  -> every primitive packet is impossible
  -> primitive normalization
  -> FLT5Target
```

The distinction between operations must remain visible in the API: unit action,
fifth-power formation, coordinate projection, norm observation, and descent are different
operations. Equalities may connect them only after the corresponding bridge theorem is
proved.
